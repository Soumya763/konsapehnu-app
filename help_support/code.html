Frequently Asked Questions

How does Konsa Pehnu recommend outfits? expand_more

Our AI looks at the pieces you've added to your Digital Wardrobe, the occasion you select, and the weather to curate outfit pairings tailored to your style.

Is my wardrobe data private? expand_more

Yes. Photos and details you add are only used to generate your personal styling recommendations and are never shared publicly.

Can I add wardrobe items without a photo? expand_more

Right now every item needs a photo (camera or upload) so our AI can tag it correctly. Manual entry without a photo is planned for a future update.

How do I retake the style quiz? expand_more

Open the Menu from any main screen and select "Retake Style Quiz" under Account & Wardrobe.

Why does the app show mock data? expand_more

Konsa Pehnu is currently a design prototype. Some content is illustrative while we build out the full experience.
