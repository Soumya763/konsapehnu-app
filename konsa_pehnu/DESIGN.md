## Brand & Style

The brand identity is rooted in **Indian-Modern Editorial Luxury**, specifically tailored for the contemporary Indian man. It moves away from loud, saturated tropes to embrace a "Sartorial Journal" aesthetic that feels tactile, bespoke, and intellectually high-end.

The design style is **Minimalism with Tactile Accents**, characterized by:
- **Warm Paper Canvas:** Replacing stark digital white with unbleached cotton and ivory tones.
- **Editorial Typography:** High-contrast pairings of classical serifs and modern sans-serifs.
- **Micro-Luxe Details:** Subtle architectural motifs like Jali lattice watermarks and diamond dividers.
- **Dynamic Elegance:** Sophisticated motion such as shimmering CTA states and smooth, floating card elevations.

The emotional response should be one of confidence and refined ease, positioning AI wardrobe management as an effortless lifestyle ritual.

## Colors

The palette is derived from organic Indian pigments—maroon, marigold, and saffron—balanced against a warm, architectural neutral base.

- **Primary (Royal Maroon):** Used for high-priority actions and brand anchors. It conveys royalty and refinement.
- **Secondary (Marigold Gold):** Reserved for editorial emphasis, italics, and active markers. It adds a shimmering, bespoke quality.
- **Tertiary (Heritage Saffron):** Used for energy and micro-details, often appearing in gradients with the gold.
- **Neutral (Paper Ivory):** The foundational surface. A warm, non-glare canvas that evokes the quality of high-end stationery.
- **Named Colors:**
    - `ink`: A deep, roasted espresso used for typography to avoid the harshness of pure black.
    - `emerald-match`: Specifically for styling synergy scores and positive health indicators.
    - `hairline`: A soft, warm grey for structural dividers that maintain the editorial flow without creating visual clutter.

## Typography

The typography system creates a dialogue between heritage and utility. **Playfair Display** provides the editorial "voice," while **Inter** ensures digital legibility and functional clarity.

- **Display & Headlines:** Use tight negative tracking and SemiBold weights to create a commanding magazine-style presence.
- **Editorial Emphasis:** Use `Playfair Display Italic` in `secondary` (Gold) color for key phrases within paragraphs to create a rhythmic, conversational brand tone.
- **Labels:** Set in all-caps Inter with generous tracking (`2px`) for category eyebrows and metadata.
- **Technical Data:** While Inter is the primary body font, use standard weights for alignment. Numeric stats should use Playfair Display for a "premium valuation" feel.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a **Fluid Grid** model with a focus on asymmetric balance, mirroring modern editorial spreads.

- **Rhythm:** A 4px baseline grid governs all spacing. Vertical section padding is generous (6vw to 8vw) to maintain an unhurried, premium pace.
- **Breakpoints:**
    - **Desktop (1024px+):** Employs asymmetric split grids (e.g., 1fr 0.9fr) for hero and feature sections. Content is capped at readable widths (max 760px for main copy blocks).
    - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Grids transition to a more balanced 12-column structure with 5vw margins.
    - **Mobile (<767px):** Single-column stacks with specific font-size scaling via `clamp()`. Multi-item carousels transition to touch-drag patterns with hidden scrollbars.
- **Alignment:** Primary copy is generally left-aligned to ground the layout, while decorative "floating" cards and badges add visual interest to the right.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is conveyed through **Tonal Layering** and **Ambient Elevation**, avoiding heavy drop shadows in favor of soft, tinted glows.

- **Tonal Tiers:** Surfaces stack from `neutral` (Paper) as the base to `cream-container` (Ivory) for elevated elements like cards and modals.
- **Ambient Shadows:** Elevations use high-diffusion, low-opacity shadows with warm tinting (e.g., `rgba(168, 137, 82, 0.15)`) to suggest a light source hitting natural paper.
- **Glassmorphism:** The primary navigation bar uses a high-blur (14px) translucent ivory backdrop to maintain context while keeping the interface feeling lightweight.
- **Interaction Depth:** Hover states utilize a subtle "lift" (-4px translation) and an increase in shadow diffusion rather than a change in border weight.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Soft and Tailored**, mirroring the precision of high-end menswear.

- **Base Radius:** UI elements like cards and inputs use a `soft` (0.25rem) radius for a structured, professional look.
- **Action Radius:** Primary and secondary buttons are always **Pill-shaped (Full)**. This distinction makes interactive elements immediately recognizable against the more rectangular, structured layout of the content.
- **In-App Previews:** Mockup elements (like phone UI previews) use higher roundedness (26px) to mimic physical hardware.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Pill-shaped, solid Maroon (`#7a1f32`) with white text. Features a 12% white sheen "shimmer" animation on hover and transitions to a Saffron-Gold gradient.
- **Secondary:** Text-only with a 1px solid underline in `ink`. Transitions to `secondary` (Gold) on hover.

### Cards
- **Editorial Cards:** 1px `hairline` border, 4px radius. Features a gold step counter (e.g., "01") and a diagonal arrow icon that animates on hover.
- **VIP/Pricing Cards:** Subtle gradient fill with a 4px multi-stop maroon-saffron accent bar at the top.

### Form Inputs
- **Base Style:** Crisp white background with 1px `hairline` border. On focus, the border shifts to Gold with a soft 3px ambient ring.
- **Labels:** Use the `label-caps` typography style for clarity.

### Feedback & Indicators
- **Match Score:** Circular or pill-shaped badges in Emerald Green, indicating wardrobe synergy.
- **Occasion Chips:** Pill-shaped with a `sandstone` outline. On hover, they invert to solid `ink` backgrounds with white text.

### Navigation
- **Header:** Sticky, frosted ivory bar with a minimalist brand lockup. The brand "question mark" is always styled in Playfair Display Italic Saffron (`#cc5527`).
