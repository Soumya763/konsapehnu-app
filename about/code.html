Our Story

Konsa Pehnu was born from a simple, everyday question every man asks himself: "What should I wear?" We set out to build a personal stylist that actually understands the modern Indian man's wardrobe — one that respects tradition, embraces contemporary fits, and takes the occasion and weather into account. Part digital wardrobe, part sartorial journal, Konsa Pehnu blends an editorial eye with everyday practicality, so getting dressed feels like a small daily ritual rather than a chore.
